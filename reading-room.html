Beyond Servitude: Sovereignty, Freedom and Political Authority in Sayyid Qutb

Sayyid Qutb's Milestones (Maʿālim fī al-ṭarīq, 1964) is one of the most influential and contested works of modern Islamic political thought. This review explores its understanding of human freedom, divine sovereignty and ʿubūdiyya, placing the text within the intellectual and political context of its time.

Reading note · Literature · Social representations

Játim: Identity, Social Order and Marginality in Raja Alem

Set in Mecca at the end of the Ottoman period, Raja Alem's novel offers a point of departure for thinking about how communities assign identities, define belonging and position those who do not fit established categories. This reading follows the tension between individual experience and collective norms, asking whether marginality is only a form of exclusion—or can also expose the limits of the social order that produces it.
